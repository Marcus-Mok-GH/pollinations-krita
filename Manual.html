Pollinations for Krita

Generate and edit images with Pollinations, paying with your own Pollen.

Connect

Click Connect, paste your publishable pk_ App Key from enter.pollinations.ai, and approve the sign-in in your browser. The token is stored privately on your machine (owner-only file on Linux/macOS, DPAPI on Windows) and never inside your document. Alternatively set the POLLINATIONS_API_KEY environment variable before starting Krita.

Generate

Choose a model from the live catalog, write a prompt, and click Generate to a new layer. With an active selection the image is placed at the selection; otherwise it covers the canvas.

Edit

Select the layer (optionally with a selection bounding the area), write what should change, pick an image-input model, and click Edit onto a new layer. The active layer/selection is sent to the edits endpoint and the result is added as a new layer above; the source is never modified.

Notes

Documents should be 8-bit RGBA for layer import. Errors (expired sign-in, low Pollen balance with a top-up hint, network failures) appear in the dock status.
